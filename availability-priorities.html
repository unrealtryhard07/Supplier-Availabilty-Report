How the numbers work

Listing
One product in one store (a product-store combination). A listing is in stock when the store holds at least one unit.
Availability
Listings in stock ÷ all listings. Every total (supplier, store, category and the grand total) is counted from listings, so it is weighted by listings rather than an average of the percentages shown.
Status
Target 85.00% and above · Below target 80.00–84.99% · Critical 79.99% and below. Percentages are rounded down, so 84.99% shows as 84% and stays amber.
Daily sales
Average units sold per day for each listing: Lifetime Qty Sold ÷ days since its first sale (at least 30 days, so new listings are not inflated). The Tableau source holds lifetime sales, not a single day’s sales.
Lost sales / day
Daily sales of the listings that are out of stock right now. This is the priority order used across the page: it is high only when a listing both sells well and is empty, so a 70% supplier that barely sells ranks below a big supplier with a smaller gap.
90% focus group
Suppliers ranked by daily sales, highest first. Each supplier’s share of total daily sales is added up in that order; every supplier up to and including the one that takes the running total past 90% is marked Focus. This group follows the store, category, sub-category and item filters, but not the supplier filter.
Left out
Items tiered TD or TP/TS in the item master, and stores with no stock anywhere.
Source
Tableau data source “Supplier’s Availability Data”.
